## Delivered contract

[DECISION] Keep schema 2. Add typed observation/lineage namespaces (`usage_observations`, `usage_corrections`, `lineage_edges`, `coverage_reports`) with `meta.observation_receipt` and typed version guards. Fresh UUID roots are distinct from route digests; explicit resume fails closed on unknown IDs. Descendant/reviewer/retry/successor bind to the original root; process/env/context/provider replacement never creates a new budget root.

Usage observations carry unit, source grade, observation/receipt times and freshness. Reconciliation uses explicit consumption identities and coverage (deltas, prefixes, corrections, epochs, parent/child overlap). Late-prefix corrections invalidate prior reconciliation and apply `snapshot − old + corrected` once per correction identity when covered versions and additive contract are proven; otherwise totals are unknown/incomplete. Missing pricing/model/allowance stay unknown — never zero or unlimited. Allowlisted metadata only; retention drops eligible detail while preserving totals/lineage. Observation recording does not grant policy authority. Mutators retain V4-06 append lock + active claim/inventory checks.
