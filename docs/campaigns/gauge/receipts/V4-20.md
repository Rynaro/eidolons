## Delivered contract

[DECISION] Keep schema 2. Add typed status-projection namespaces (`status_projections`, `status_client_receipts`, `status_events`) with `meta.status_receipt` and typed version guards. Do not replace `delivery.go`; add an observational status projection layer over V4-15 `InspectSnapshot` / delivery state and V4-11/V4-12 reservation/cancel observations. Delivery and verification states are reported separately (implemented/runnable/cancelled/partial/blocked vs checks-passed/review-pending/released/stale-evidence). Participation distinguishes methods, worker identities, and context-separation evidence (one maker many methods vs separate specialist/checker; no fictional team). Unsupported/stale provider quota surfaces as plain-text limitations with unknown buckets and no invented percentages (advisory-only / outside-controller). Policy inspect/preview never dispatches model work; denied escalation is recorded; preview preserves outstanding reservations. Cancellation projections separate request acknowledgement, confirmed stop, and accounting reconciliation — process may continue after ack with late usage; no premature done/refund. Shared `gauge-status@1` contract is consumed by CLI and a fixture client (no-GUI/no-color); unsupported contract versions reject authority-bearing mutations while retaining a compatibility diagnostic; read-only diagnostics do not dispatch. Terminal/green badge ≠ acceptance. Status/preview cannot refill budgets or create live-evidence claims.

Zero real network/model. Live qualification remains fail-closed. Bash 3.2 legacy CLI unchanged; status commands are opt-in Go only. No V4-21 / V4-16+.
