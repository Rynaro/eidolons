Failed staging/switch/smoke recovers usable previous installation plus sessions/settings/candidates/evidence/pending reservations with repeat-migration safety. Release candidates distinguish docs-only vs code/behavior; smoke success never replaces missing authorized live evidence. Insufficient promotion evidence or missing approval leaves behavioral defaults unchanged; prepared reports never authorize tag/merge/publication. Unmigrated supported consumers block retirement; even complete evidence still refuses archival in this package. Host version drift invalidates cancellation/usage/tool-boundary catalogue qualification until rechecked. Readiness reports correctness, managed-operation qualification, and performance promotion as separate decisions with no composite green badge; governance-only improvements are labeled and cannot claim measured speed/cost superiority; optional experiments may remain deferred. Strategy/method rollback retains task lineage, evidence history, and outstanding obligations under the still-authorized contract without stale acceptance, budget refill, or wider authority.
