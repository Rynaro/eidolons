**Closed — WONTFIX-with-rationale, plus a shipped gate (v2.0.2).**

**The gate is real and it goes red.** `evals/corpus_scaling_gate.py` plants a ground-truth
record whose BM25 rank exceeds `candidate_k` and shows it dropped:
- large corpus: `M=60`, `candidate_k=30`, `len(sparse_ranking)==30` **exactly** (so the fetch
  was genuinely censored, not merely small), `planted_recovered: false` → RED
- small-corpus control: `M=5`, `planted_recovered: true` → GREEN

Single layer, asserted — because `retrieve.py`'s fetch loop both iterates layers and truncates
at `candidate_k`, so layer-major ordering (#45) and truncation (this issue) produce the *same*
symptom through different causes. A single-layer store makes ordering structurally impossible
as a cause. `FETCH_WIDTH_FLOOR` is read and asserted unchanged at 10, never written.

**Why the fix is not shipped.** What a synthetic fixture can prove is that *some* wider fetch
recovers *the record its author planted*. Its ground truth is stipulated, so it cannot
adjudicate between `k*3`, `k*5`, `c·log(n_scoped)` or `n_scoped/2` — every one of them
"passes" if you make it wide enough, and none of them is priced for the latency it buys.

v1.11.0 already shipped a cosmetic `max(k*3, FETCH_WIDTH_FLOOR)` link that is a no-op at
defaults and closed this issue on it. That is why it is open a second time, and repeating it
would be worse than leaving it open.

`candidate_k` stays a constant multiple of `k` by design. The `explain.fusion` telemetry
needed to see the ceiling bind is already shipped.

**Reopen condition:** `explain.fusion` data from a real store showing the ceiling binding in
practice — a response curve on non-stipulated ground truth. Reopen with that measurement and
the scaling law becomes decidable; without it, any constant written here is a guess wearing a
number.
