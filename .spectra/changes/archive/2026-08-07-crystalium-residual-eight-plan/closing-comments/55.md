**Closed — the sub-1.0 `fusion_weight_derived` band is left FORMALLY UNSUPPORTED (the issue's
own second option). Recorded in `config.py` (v2.0.2).**

**What shipped:**
- A statement in `config.py` that values below 1.0 are legal, **unsupported**, and will remain
  uncharacterised until a fixture with non-stipulated ground truth exists. No sub-1.0 value is
  presented as a supported precision dial (C-9).
- `evals/weight_discrimination.py` — a weight-discriminating fixture that genuinely
  discriminates: at `fusion_weight_derived ∈ {0.90, 0.95, 1.00}` it produces **2 distinct
  outcomes**, with the crossover at the stated arithmetic `w/(60+r_A)` vs `1/(60+r_B)` →
  `w* = 61/62 ≈ 0.968`. The degeneracy this issue reported is gone.
- An eval-notes pointer recording that the **retrieval** gate is the informative fixture for
  weight sweeps and the **fusion** gate is not (target/Z at k=2 cannot express it), so the next
  sweep does not vacuously "pass" on the wrong fixture.

**The fixture is labelled as an instrument, not as characterisation.** Its docstring's first
paragraph states it is the DP-1(b) re-check oracle for #42 and explicitly **not** band
characterisation, and that string is asserted by a test. This matters because #52's finding was
an artefact that *presents* as evidence for something it cannot support — a future reader
finding a weight-sweep fixture and citing it as "the band is characterised" would be the same
error one axis over.

**Why item 1 is not closable as a quality measurement:** what the fixture produces is a
response curve of the combiner's rank arithmetic, which `config.py:296-312` already documents.
It cannot say whether demoting the derived-only record is *good*, because the fixture author
decided which record is relevant.

**Item 2 is dropped, and this is a real reduction in evidence.** The issue asked to "re-run the
§D2 bitwise identity harness". That harness does not exist — there is no `evals/d2_identity.py`
and no `d2-identity` subcommand; the only trace is a recorded RESULT in a `config.py:292-293`
comment. "Re-run" was never possible. The identity property therefore continues to rest on the
recorded structural argument (candidate generation changed, combiner arithmetic did not), which
survives #42 and #45 — not on fresh evidence.

**Forward obligation, now recorded in `config.py`:** any future change touching combiner
arithmetic (`weighted_rrf_merge_scored`, the RRF constant, or `fusion_weight_*` semantics) MUST
build and run a d2-identity harness — 20 in-process comparisons, `max_abs_diff == 0.0` at
`w=1.0`, plus a 1-ULP perturbation red-check — as a precondition of that change.
